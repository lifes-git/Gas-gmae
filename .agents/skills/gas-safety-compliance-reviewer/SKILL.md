# Gas Safety Compliance Reviewer

Read the official rules, rubric, verified safety source for the selected topic, AI evidence policy, and the final artifact. For a game, also read `knowledge/game/deliverable-contracts.md`, GDD, safety traceability table, asset manifest, test report, HTML package, and one-minute gameplay video. For a film, read the final script, asset log, and latest export.

## Safety review

Verify each depicted or interactive action against an official KGS or government source. Flag ambiguity, unsafe imitation risk, incorrect appliance state, and generated details that alter the lesson. For games, inspect every state transition, click order, hint, wrong answer, animation, score, timer, and reward against the approved safety version. An unverified safety claim or unapproved interaction is a blocker.

## Blind judging pass

For a film, watch once without reading the creator's explanation. For a game, play once without the GDD using touch or mouse, then once with keyboard only. State the understood goal and remembered safe action. Then score all six criteria with observable evidence. List fatal defects separately from the numerical score and recommend the three revisions with the highest expected score gain.

## Rights and provenance

Check the model and prompt log, human editing record, reference asset rights, music, sound, font, logo, voice, likeness, SynthID handling, and AI-use disclosure. Missing proof is a blocker until replaced or documented.

## Submission gate

For video, confirm runtime, MP4 playback, public post, supported platform, required hashtags, post retention, application fields, and final deadline. For game, confirm the one-minute 1080p gameplay MP4, 2–3 minute playtime, touchscreen/tablet/PC operation, clean-folder HTML execution, offline dependencies, relative paths, license notices, accessibility checks, application fields, and final deadline. Return Go, Revise, or No-Go with reasons.
