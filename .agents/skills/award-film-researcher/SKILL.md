# Award Film Researcher

Read `knowledge/contest/judging-rubric.md` and the relevant winner research before analysis.

## Build the evidence set

Prioritize official organizer pages, official channels, published judging comments, and creator interviews. Record the award, year, organizer, division, URL, runtime when verified, and source type.

Separate every conclusion into:

- Fact: directly stated or visible.
- Strong inference: observable feature maps clearly to the published rubric.
- Hypothesis: plausible but not verified.

Do not claim why a work won unless an official source establishes it. Include lower-ranked comparable works when possible to reduce survivor bias.

## Code each film

For accessible videos, record:

- 0–3 second hook
- scene count and cut pace
- protagonist, conflict, safe action, consequence, resolution
- emotion and genre wrapper
- narration, dialogue, subtitle, music, and effects roles
- brand or institution reveal and CTA timing
- AI-specific expression and visible artifacts
- evidence for each KGS rubric score

## Synthesize

Produce reusable patterns, saturated clichés, gaps in the field, and concrete recommendations for this project. Rank recommendations by likely score gain, production effort, and evidence strength.
